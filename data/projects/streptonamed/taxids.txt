# The genus Streptococcus without the two branches of it that carry no names.
#
# WHY. Under the LCA update a genome the taxonomy cannot place is not neutral but a sink. A k-mer
# shared between S. pneumoniae and an unnamed draft has its lowest common ancestor at the *genus*,
# because the draft hangs off a sibling branch of the entire mitis lineage; one such genome suffices
# to lift a species' k-mers clear out of it. It is not a small effect here: in the database built
# from 1301 alone, S. pneumoniae keeps 397 k-mers of its own where S. oralis keeps 206,674, and in
# 83 clinical samples not one classification ever landed below it. The 468 genomes under
# `unclassified Streptococcus' hold 45.6 million k-mers between them, 109 of them more than 100,000
# each, so they are whole genomes rather than fragments.
#
# This project is the control that tests exactly that: same genus, same configuration, only the two
# unnamed branches left out. If they are the cause, the k-mers at S. pneumoniae rise by orders of
# magnitude.
#
# Nothing else is dropped. The genus has 147 children, of which 145 carry a name -- 140 species and
# five species groups -- and all of them stay.
