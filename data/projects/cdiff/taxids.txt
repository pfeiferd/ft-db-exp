# Clostridioides difficile, one species and nothing else.
#
# Renamed from Clostridium difficile in 2016 (Lawson et al.); NCBI carries the synonyms
# Clostridium difficile and Peptoclostridium difficile under the same tax id, so nothing has to be
# listed twice.
#
# Every other database of this project requests a genus or a whole category and is refined between
# a genus and its species. This one requests a single species and is refined *below* it, which is
# the regime the paper has not otherwise covered -- see config.properties for why C. difficile is
# the organism to do it with.
