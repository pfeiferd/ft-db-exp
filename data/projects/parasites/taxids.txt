# THE GENUS-LEVEL `unclassified' BUCKETS ARE LEFT OUT, below. This needs `refseq.updateScope=
# allButExcluded' in config.properties to do its real work: the lines here keep those genomes out of
# the database, the config line also stops them from raising the k-mers of the species that stay.
#
# WHY. Under the LCA update a genome the taxonomy cannot place is not neutral but a sink. A k-mer it
# shares with a named species of the same genus has its lowest common ancestor at the *genus*, so the
# species loses it -- to a genome that is not even in the database once these lines are here. Each
# bucket below is a child of a genus this project asks for and holds nothing but unnamed genomes.
#
# WHAT IS NOT LEFT OUT, and must not be. `incertae sedis' nodes hold *named* species and are a
# statement about where those species sit, not a bin of drafts. Nor is any bucket whose members carry
# real names: in the viral project all 826 species under such nodes are named viruses, so the same
# rule would delete content there. The criterion is the members, never the node's name.
#
