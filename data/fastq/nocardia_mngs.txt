# The fourteen clinical metagenomes of ENA study PRJEB34974, "Nocardiosis identified by NGS
# approach", Huashan Hospital / Fudan University, public since 2019-11-08. The study behind them is
#   Zhang Y, Ai J-W, Cui P, Zhang W-H, et al.: "Rapid Detection of Nocardia by Next-Generation
#   Sequencing." Front Cell Infect Microbiol 2020;10:13.  (PMC7040243)
# which names this accession in its data-availability statement.
#
# They are the study's NG group: ten patients whose nocardiosis was found by sequencing, sampled as
# five CSF, four BALF, two sputum, two lung tissue and one cutaneous pus. Cases 6, 9 and 10 
# contribute more than one sample, which is why fourteen runs come from ten patients.
#
# BGISEQ-500, 50-100 bp SINGLE END, shotgun (200-300 bp fragments), host-depleted against hg38
# before deposit. ENA labels every run `library_strategy=AMPLICON'; that is wrong -- the paper
# describes shotgun metagenomics -- and it is why a search filtering on strategy does not find this
# study. Do not trust the field.
#
# WHAT THIS DATA CAN AND CANNOT CARRY. The per-sample reference standard is complete: Table S4 of
# the paper gives the species, the specimen and the Nocardia read count for every one of the
# fourteen, and it is transcribed into data/projects/nocardia/ground_truth.csv. But the signal is
# small and very unevenly spread -- 29,175 Nocardia reads over the whole cohort, of which two
# samples hold 82 per cent, and two others hold 2 and 17 reads. Report this cohort case by case with
# the read count beside every claim. A mean over samples holding 2 and 16,143 reads is not a
# measurement, it is those two samples wearing a cohort's clothes.
#
# NO RUN-TO-CASE MAPPING EXISTS. All fourteen runs carry the identical sample title `HS-nocardia',
# ENA's read counts are whole-sample totals while Table S4's are Nocardia-specific, and nothing
# joins them. Accession order is not evidence of case order. The comparison that needs no mapping:
# the species called across the fourteen runs must be exactly the six of the standard, with the
# multiplicities farcinica 3, brasiliensis 3, terpenica 3, africana 2, otitidiscaviarum 2,
# cyriacigeorgica 1.
#
# The study's other two groups are NOT deposited -- neither the sixteen culture-positive samples nor
# the fifty-five Nocardia-negative ones, which would have been the specificity control. There is no
# negative control for this database.
#
#   mvn exec:exec@fastqdl -Dname=nocardia -Dfqmap=nocardia_mngs.txt
#
