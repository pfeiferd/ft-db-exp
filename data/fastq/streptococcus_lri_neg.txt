# The six process extraction negatives of ENA study PRJEB30781, the study whose 83 clinical runs are
# in streptococcus_lri_real.txt beside this file. Same protocol, no sample: what the saponin
# host-depletion and library preparation put into a run on their own.
#
# They are tiny -- 510 reads across all six, i.e. under a hundred each -- which is the point. Any
# Streptococcus the refined database reports here is reagent contamination or a false positive, and
# with counts this low the two are distinguishable by eye. They are the specificity control for the
# real-data part of the case study, playing the role that viral_saliva_specificity.csv plays for the
# viral database.
#
#   mvn exec:exec@fastqdl -Dname=streptococcus -Dfqmap=streptococcus_lri_neg.txt
#
# ENA numbers them 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 -- there is no number 4 in the study.
#
